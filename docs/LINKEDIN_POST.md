# LinkedIn draft

I built a cybersecurity maze game with Codex.

It’s called Ghost Protocol. You guide a small maintenance drone through five facilities, collect access keys, avoid moving sentries, and recover a package.

The catch: the sentries can get a copy of your Vault key.

Getting away means crossing the gate and revoking that access behind you. Later missions give you a separate Transit key, so shutting down the compromised permission can leave your exit working.

I directed the gameplay, visuals, and learning experience. Codex handled implementation and verification as we worked through the revisions.

Some of the most useful changes came from actually playing it: making the mazes feel like mazes, removing an interrupting key-choice dialog, explaining the floor devices, and putting the lockdown switch somewhere a player would notice. Apparently “just lock the door” needs a very clear button.

The result is a small playable model of credentials, expiry, revocation, and access scope. There’s an introduction and optional practice for anyone new to the concepts.
